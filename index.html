Yosef ClipForge

Yosef ClipForge is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs on the owner's own computer, turns stream recordings into 1080×1920 vertical clips (trimming, layouts, subtitles, text overlays) and uploads the finished clips to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through each platform's official API.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit - to connect the owner's TikTok account and show its name and avatar inside the tool.
  • Content Posting API - to send a finished clip to TikTok, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a direct post, only when the owner presses Publish.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and nobody else's account or data is involved.
